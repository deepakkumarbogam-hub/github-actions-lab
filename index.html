My Chinnu,

I don't know exactly when you became such a huge part of my life. Maybe it happened slowly. Maybe it happened without either of us noticing.

But somewhere along the way, you stopped being just someone I love and became someone I can't imagine my life without.

You became the person I look for in my notifications. The person I want to tell things to. The person whose happiness matters to me. The person I miss when the day gets quiet.

"I don't need you to be beside me every second. I just need to know that no matter how far apart we are, we're still choosing each other."

Chinnu, I know long distance isn't always easy. There will be days when we miss each other more than usual. Days when a call isn't enough. Days when we wish we could just be together.

But I want you to know that I am not scared of the distance.

Because distance is temporary. What I feel for you isn't.

I want to see you achieve your dreams. I want to see you become stronger, happier and more confident. I want to be there cheering for you when things go right, and holding you together when things don't.

I want to know all the versions of you. The happy you. The tired you. The angry you. The silly you. The emotional you. The girl who goes to the gym and gives her best. The girl who sometimes needs someone to simply say, "It's okay, I'm here."

And I want you to know that I am here.

Even from far away.

I may not be able to hold your hand today. But I can still hold your heart carefully.

I may not be able to see you whenever I want. But I will still look forward to every moment I get with you.

And one day, I hope all these video calls, countdowns, missed hugs and "I wish you were here" become stories we tell about the time when we were far apart.

Until then...

I'll keep loving you from here.

I'll keep missing you.

I'll keep choosing you.

And I'll keep waiting for the day when the distance between us is finally nothing more than a memory.

Yours,
Bebo ❤️
